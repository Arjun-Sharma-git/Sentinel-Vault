# Employee Handbook 2026

## Paid time off
Every full-time employee gets 25 days of paid time off per year plus 10 public holidays. Unused PTO rolls over up to 5 days into the next year. Interns accrue 1.5 days per month worked.

## Office hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours, work whenever suits you and your team.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days a week. Fully remote arrangements need manager and HR approval.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a 1,500 USD yearly learning budget for courses, books and conferences.
