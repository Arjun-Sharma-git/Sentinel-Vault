# Benefits 2026

## Health
Medical, dental and vision insurance start on your first day. The company pays 90% of premiums.

## Retirement
We match 401(k) contributions up to 4% of salary.

## Wellbeing
Every employee gets two wellbeing days per year. They are separate from paid time off and don't roll over. Gym membership is reimbursed up to 50 USD per month.
