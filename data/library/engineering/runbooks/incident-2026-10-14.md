# Postmortem: Payments outage, 14 October 2026

## Summary
Card payments failed for 47 minutes between 09:12 and 09:59 UTC.

## Root cause
An expired TLS certificate on the internal payments gateway caused every call from the checkout service to fail. Certificate expiry alerts had been routed to an archived channel.

## Action items
Automate certificate renewal with cert-manager, and route expiry alerts to the on-call pager.
